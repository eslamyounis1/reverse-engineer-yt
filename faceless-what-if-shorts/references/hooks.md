# Hook DNA

Evidence: 5/5 references open with a 2nd-person hypothetical question. It lasts 1–3 s (mean 2.2 s),
runs 10–14 words (mean 11.8), and plays over a frame that already shows the avatar inside the
premise. "Day one" follows at 1.7–3.0 s in 5/5 references.
Confidence: **High**.

## Formula

```
[OPENER] [YOU + VERB] [EXTREME CONDITION][, optional bound]?
```

| Slot | Allowed values | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| OPENER | "What would happen if" (default) · "What if" (variant) | 4/5 · 1/5 |
| YOU + VERB | "you were born as / in", "you were raised by", "you were stranded in", "you woke up as / in", "you had to survive" | born ×2, raised ×2, stranded ×1 |
| EXTREME CONDITION | one displacement axis pushed to its limit (table below) | 5/5 |
| Bound | "for five days", "for 24 hours", "until you turn 30". Required for **bounded** premises | 1/5 |

Constraints:

- **10–14 words** and **≤ 2.5 s** when spoken at the narration rate. Count the words.
- Exactly one question mark. No preamble ("Have you ever wondered…"). No "Imagine".
- The next spoken words are the first time stamp ("Day one." / "Hour one."). Leave 0.2–0.3 s
  of silence between them.
- A Day-one line that **subverts the expected version** of the moment works well. Pattern:
  "Day one: you're not [normal], you're [premise version]."

## Displacement axes (premise generator)

Pick one axis and push it to its extreme. Then check the premise against the gates below.

| Axis | Reference evidence | New-territory examples (do not reuse the reference premises) |
|---|---|---|
| Status / wealth | raised by a billionaire | raised as heir to a failing royal family; born into a family that owns a whole island nation |
| Era | ancient Rome; year 3000 | born in medieval plague-era London; born on the first generation ship to another star |
| Environment | Alaskan wilderness, 5 days | stranded on an Antarctic research base all winter; living 100 m under the ocean for a year |
| Society / group | most dangerous tribe | raised inside a deep-sea salvage crew; raised by a travelling circus in the 1920s |
| Body / biology | (implied by avatar) | born unable to feel pain; you stop ageing at 20 |
| Rule change | (R4 twist) | the sun never sets where you're born; money doesn't exist |

**Premise gates.** All must pass.

1. **Visual in frame 1:** the hook image can show the condition with no text.
2. **Five chapters of escalation:** you can list 5 rising rites of passage or stakes.
3. **A turn exists:** there is a natural inversion, extreme or twist for chapter 4/5.
4. **Original:** not one of the five reference premises or a near-copy (billionaire upbringing,
   Roman slave/gladiator, born in year 3000, 5 days in Alaska, raised by a dangerous tribe).
5. **Safe and factual:** no real living people. No glorified real-world atrocity; historical
   suffering is shown with dignity, not gore. Facts must be checkable.

When proposing premises, give 3 in one line each:
`Hook question | type (lifespan/bounded) | chapter units | turn idea`.

## Hook frame (frame 1) recipe

- The avatar is **in** the premise at the earliest life stage, **in motion or under pressure**:
  being carried, dragged or delivered; the storm; arrival; first contact.
- Shot scale: Medium (3/5) or Wide (2/5). Use Wide only when the setting itself is the hook.
- One premise-defining object or person behind the avatar: a chef and chandelier, guards on a
  ship, robotic arms, a seaplane.
- Make it the most polished frame of the video: GPT Image 2.5 flare at `xhigh` or `max`.
- Optional (1/5, Medium confidence): a 1 s flash-forward shot of the avatar's most spectacular
  later state, cut in right after the question and before "Day one".

## Title patterns

The upload title mirrors the hook question, shortened. Examples:
`What if you were born under the ocean?` · `What would happen if you lived on a generation ship?`
