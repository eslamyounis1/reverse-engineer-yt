# Research: born under the ocean in 2200

Hypothetical future events are invented. Facts about the ocean, pressure and the human body are checked:

| Script detail | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "a hundred metres underwater" + domes | Pressure rises ~1 atm per 10 m of seawater, so 100 m is ~11 atm. Real undersea habitats exist; Aquarius sits at 19 m off Key Largo. | NOAA Ocean Explorer, Aquarius; Wikipedia Aquarius Reef Base |
| "The air is helium. Your first cry is a squeak." | Saturation divers deeper than ~50 m breathe helium mixes, which raise voice pitch to cartoonish "Donald Duck" speech. | gCaptain, saturation diving |
| "Red is the first colour the ocean swallows." / "your kite turns grey" | Red light is absorbed in the top ~10 m, orange by ~40 m, yellow before 100 m; only blue and green go deep. About 1% of surface light is left at 100 m. | NOAA Ocean Service, How far does light travel |
| "sun lamps keep your bones strong" | Without sunlight, vitamin D drops (submariners lose ~40% over a patrol); UV/supplements are used to protect bones. | DTIC, Vitamin D supplementation in submariners |
| "four days sealed in a decompression chamber" | Saturation divers decompress once, slowly. Aquarius at 19 m needs 17 h; decompression from ~100 m saturation takes several days. | NOAA Aquarius; gCaptain |
| Kites in the deep current power the city | Real tidal "underwater kites" fly figure-eights in ocean currents to generate electricity. | Wikipedia, Minesto (Deep Green) |

The supporting cast are roles (parents, nurses, teacher, kids). No real people, brands or logos.
