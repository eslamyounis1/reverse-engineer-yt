# Research: "What If You Were Born on Mars in 2100?"

Real facts about Mars that the script uses. The 2100 colony itself is hypothetical; every
physical claim below is real and sourced.

| # | Fact used | Script line | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surface gravity is about 38% of Earth's (0.38 g) | "You weigh just over a third of an Earth kid." | [NASA Mars Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html), [Universe Today](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/gravity-on-mars) |
| 2 | 1 / 0.38 ≈ 2.6, so on Earth a Mars-raised person would feel about 2.6× heavier than they are used to. Their mass is unchanged; "feel" is the precise verb | "On Earth, you'd feel nearly three times heavier." | derived from #1 |
| 3 | Surface pressure is about 6.4 mbar (4.0–8.7), roughly 0.6% of Earth's, far below the Armstrong limit (~63 mbar). Exposed fluids such as saliva and tears boil; blood inside the vessels does not, because blood pressure keeps it liquid. Consciousness is lost in about 10–15 s from lack of oxygen | "Outside, without a suit, you'd pass out in seconds as your saliva boils." | [NASA Mars Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html), [Atmosphere of Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmosphere_of_Mars) |
| 4 | Surface radiation is about 0.67 mSv/day (Curiosity RAD), so long-term habitats are expected to use regolith shielding. "Three metres of dirt" is a plausible design choice, not a measured requirement | "You're born under three metres of dirt that blocks radiation." | [SwRI / Curiosity RAD](https://www.swri.org/newsroom/press-releases/swri-scientists-publish-first-radiation-measurements-the-surface-of-mars), [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2013/12/131209181133.htm) |
| 5 | One-way Earth–Mars light delay is about 3–22 minutes depending on orbital positions. 12 minutes is a mid value. Only a radio message travels this fast; physical cargo takes months by ship, so the seed is brought **with** the parents, not sent in reply | "Your first cry, sent by radio, takes twelve minutes to reach Earth. Your parents carried one gift from Earth…" | [NASA ASCEND paper](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20220013418/downloads/ASCEND-Communication%20Delays,%20Disruptions,%20and%20Blackouts%20for%20Crewed%20Mars%20Missions.pdf) |
| 6 | Daytime sky is butterscotch or tan; sunsets have a blue glow around the Sun (dust scattering) | "The noon sky is butterscotch. The sunsets glow blue." | [JPL: Mars sunset clip](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/mars-sunset-clip-from-opportunity-tells-dusty-tale/) |
| 7 | Planet-encircling dust storms happen. The 2018 storm ran about June–September, blocked sunlight, and ended solar-powered Opportunity | "A dust storm swallows the whole planet for months. The solar panels go dark." | [2018 Mars global dust storm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Mars_global_dust_storm), [JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/opportunity-hunkers-down-during-dust-storm/) |
| 8 | A Martian day ("sol") is 24 h 39 m 35 s | "sol after sol" | [Timekeeping on Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timekeeping_on_Mars) |
| 9 | Efficient Earth–Mars transfer windows recur about every 26 months | "Launch windows open every twenty-six months." | [Launch window](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Launch_window) |
| 10 | No human data exists on development in 0.38 g. Researchers expect reduced bone and muscle development, and that returning to 1 g could be a serious problem | "You grew up in Mars gravity… The ship leaves without you." (presented as one possible future, not a certainty) | [ScienceDirect: neuroplasticity in the first Martians](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S3117347026000751), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/33082-would-humans-born-on-mars-grow-taller-than-earthlings-.html) |

Supporting facts gathered but not in the script:

- Regolith contains toxic perchlorates (0.5–1%). That is why the seed grows in a tin cup of
  imported or treated soil and in a greenhouse.
- Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.
- A Mars year is 687 Earth days. The chapter years are **Earth years**, which is how viewers
  count age.

**Accuracy notes:**

- The medical-scan outcome in chapter 4 is a dramatised hypothesis consistent with current
  research (#10), not an established fact. The narration frames it as what happens to "you" in
  this story.
- "The only green for kilometres" is a story claim about this colony, not about Mars.
